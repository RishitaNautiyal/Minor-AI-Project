About OffGrid

Helping students and families stay safer, smarter, and healthier online.

The internet is a powerful part of everyday life. We use it to learn, connect, create, and have fun, but being online also comes with risks.

OffGrid is a digital safety project designed to help students and families understand online risks, identify potential threats, and develop healthier technology habits.

Our goal is simple: make digital safety easier to understand and easier to practice.

What OffGrid Provides

OffGrid brings practical digital-safety tools and guidance together in one place, helping users identify online risks and make safer choices.

🔎 Scam Checker

Check suspicious URLs and messages for common scam and security risk indicators before interacting with them.

🎭 Deepfake Checker

Analyze uploaded images through a demonstration-based deepfake checker and learn why manipulated media can be difficult to identify.

🚩 Cyberbullying Flag

Identify potentially harmful, abusive, or bullying language and recognize when an online conversation may need attention.

⏱️ Screen Time

Build awareness of digital habits and encourage a healthier balance between screen time, study, sleep, and everyday life.

📋 Activity History

Review previous checks and activities in one place to better understand your digital-safety habits over time.

🛡️ Safety Guidance

Get simple, practical guidance on protecting personal information, handling suspicious content, responding to online bullying, and using technology responsibly.

Why We Built OffGrid

Students and families spend a significant amount of time online. Along with the benefits of technology, they may encounter scams, cyberbullying, manipulated content, privacy risks, and unhealthy screen habits.

OffGrid was created to bring these challenges together in one simple and approachable platform.

Instead of making digital safety complicated, we focus on awareness, practical tools, clear guidance, and responsible technology use.

Our Approach

Awareness → Check → Think → Act

CheckReview suspicious links, messages, and content.

Our Vision

We believe technology should improve our lives, not take control of them.

OffGrid aims to help students and families become more confident, aware, and responsible digital citizens while building a healthier relationship with technology.

Stay aware. Stay balanced. Stay safer online.
